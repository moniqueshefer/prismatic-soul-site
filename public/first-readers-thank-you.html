First Readers

Thank you for wanting to read it.

One small step first — there is a confirmation email on its way to you now. Click the link inside it, and the chapters will follow.

What happens next

  1. Check your inbox. A confirmation email should arrive within a minute or two. If you cannot see it, look in spam or promotions — confirmation emails often land there.
  2. Click the link inside it. Nothing is sent until you do, so this step matters.
  3. The chapters follow. They go out by hand rather than automatically, so please allow a working day.

In the meantime, there is plenty here to be going on with.

  • The practices — free guided audio for a system that is revved, flat, or somewhere you cannot name yet.
  • The Blog — longer essays on attention, the nervous system, and what the research does and does not show.
  • The retreats — week-long immersions at a chateau in the Gers.

And if something in the chapters lands, rattles, surprises or moves you, write to me. That is the whole invitation.
